Privacy policy

CLYFORM channel sync — last updated 3 October 2026

CLYFORM channel sync is a private, single-user tool. The only person who signs in is its owner, reading data for a YouTube channel they own. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

With the user's consent, and read-only: metadata and aggregated analytics for the user's own channel. No personal data about viewers is accessed; YouTube provides aggregated figures only.

Storage and sharing

Data and credentials are stored locally on the owner's own device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and nothing is sold, published, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and revoking access

Data is kept locally until deleted by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions, which stops all further access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
